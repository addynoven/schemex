# Sukanya Samriddhi Yojana

## 1. Overview & Objective
Small deposit savings scheme for girl child with high government-backed interest (8.2% p.a.) and triple tax exemption (EEE).

## 2. Benefits & Financial Assistance
- **High Compounded Returns & Tax Exemption:** 8.2% annual interest compounded yearly, completely exempt from income tax under Section 80C.

## 3. Eligibility Criteria (Deterministic Rules)
- `gender eq female`
- `age lte 10`

## 4. Required Documents Checklist
- [**Birth Certificate**](../../../documents/general-compliance/birth-certificate.md) (Mandatory): Birth certificate of the girl child
- [**Parent Aadhaar Card**](../../../documents/identity/parent-aadhaar-card.md) (Mandatory): Identity and address proof of legal guardian

## 5. Application Procedure
1. Verify that your citizen profile satisfies the eligibility rules listed above.
2. Ensure you have the required documents uploaded and verified in your Document Vault.
3. Access the official application portal: [https://www.indiapost.gov.in](https://www.indiapost.gov.in).
4. Submit the application and save your acknowledgement number.
