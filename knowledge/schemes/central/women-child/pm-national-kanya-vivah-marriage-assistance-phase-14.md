# PM National Kanya Vivah & Marriage Assistance Phase 14

## 1. Overview & Objective
Central flagship program: Direct financial grant for marriage of daughters from economically weaker families. Administered by Ministry of Agriculture & Farmers Welfare, Government of India.

## 2. Benefits & Financial Assistance
- **National Kanya Vivah & Marriage Assistance:** Direct financial grant for marriage of daughters from economically weaker families. (₹51,000)

## 3. Eligibility Criteria (Deterministic Rules)
- `gender eq Female`

## 4. Required Documents Checklist
- [**Aadhaar Card**](../../../documents/identity/aadhaar-card.md) (Mandatory): National Aadhaar Card for verification
- [**Ration Card**](../../../documents/income-wealth/ration-card.md) (Mandatory): National Ration Card for verification
- [**Bank Passbook**](../../../documents/income-wealth/bank-passbook.md) (Mandatory): National Bank Passbook for verification
- [**Income Certificate**](../../../documents/income-wealth/income-certificate.md) (Optional): National Income Certificate for verification

## 5. Application Procedure
1. Verify that your citizen profile satisfies the eligibility rules listed above.
2. Ensure you have the required documents uploaded and verified in your Document Vault.
3. Access the official application portal: [https://dbtbharat.gov.in/schemes/pm-national-kanya-vivah-marriage-assistance-phase-14](https://dbtbharat.gov.in/schemes/pm-national-kanya-vivah-marriage-assistance-phase-14).
4. Submit the application and save your acknowledgement number.
