Pradhan Mantri Krishi Sinchayee Yojana (PMKSY)

Per Drop More Crop • Department of Water Resources, River Development & Ganga Rejuvenation

Primary Direct Benefit
Up to ₹1,50,000 (55% - 90% Subsidy Rate)

Subsidized micro-irrigation hardware (drip pipes, drippers, filtration, sprinkler systems) directly transferred or vendor disbursed.

About The Scheme

PMKSY has been formulated with the vision of extending the coverage of irrigation 'Har Khet Ko Pani' and improving water use efficiency 'More Crop Per Drop' in a focused manner with end-to-end solutions on source creation and distribution.

Launch Year 2015
Applicability All Indian States
Landholding Cap Up to 5 Hectares
