# Pradhan Mantri Kisan Samman Nidhi

## 1. Overview & Objective
Direct income support of ₹6,000 per year paid in three equal installments to small and marginal farmer families.

## 2. Benefits & Financial Assistance
- **₹6,000 Annual Direct Cash Benefit:** ₹2,000 transferred every four months directly into Aadhaar-linked bank accounts.

## 3. Eligibility Criteria (Deterministic Rules)
- `occupation eq farmer`
- `annual_income lte 200000`

## 4. Required Documents Checklist
- [**Aadhaar Card**](../../../documents/identity/aadhaar-card.md) (Mandatory): Mandatory identity proof
- [**Bank Passbook**](../../../documents/income-wealth/bank-passbook.md) (Mandatory): Bank account details linked to Aadhaar
- [**Land Records**](../../../documents/property-land/land-records.md) (Mandatory): Khasra / Khatauni land ownership paper

## 5. Application Procedure
1. Verify that your citizen profile satisfies the eligibility rules listed above.
2. Ensure you have the required documents uploaded and verified in your Document Vault.
3. Access the official application portal: [https://pmkisan.gov.in](https://pmkisan.gov.in).
4. Submit the application and save your acknowledgement number.
