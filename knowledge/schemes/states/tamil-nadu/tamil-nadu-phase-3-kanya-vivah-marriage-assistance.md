# Tamil Nadu Phase-3 Kanya Vivah & Marriage Assistance

## 1. Overview & Objective
Direct financial grant for marriage of daughters from economically weaker families. Implemented by Tamil Nadu government for citizen welfare.

## 2. Benefits & Financial Assistance
- **Kanya Vivah & Marriage Assistance:** Direct financial grant for marriage of daughters from economically weaker families. (₹51,000)

## 3. Eligibility Criteria (Deterministic Rules)
- `state eq Tamil Nadu`
- `gender eq Female`

## 4. Required Documents Checklist
- [**Aadhaar Card**](../../../documents/identity/aadhaar-card.md) (Mandatory): Official Aadhaar Card for verification
- [**Ration Card**](../../../documents/income-wealth/ration-card.md) (Mandatory): Official Ration Card for verification
- [**Bank Passbook**](../../../documents/income-wealth/bank-passbook.md) (Mandatory): Official Bank Passbook for verification
- [**Income Certificate**](../../../documents/income-wealth/income-certificate.md) (Optional): Official Income Certificate for verification

## 5. Application Procedure
1. Verify that your citizen profile satisfies the eligibility rules listed above.
2. Ensure you have the required documents uploaded and verified in your Document Vault.
3. Access the official application portal: [https://serviceonline.gov.in/tamil-nadu](https://serviceonline.gov.in/tamil-nadu).
4. Submit the application and save your acknowledgement number.
