# Pradhan Mantri Ujjwala Yojana 2.0

## 1. Overview & Objective
Deposit-free LPG connection for adult women of poor households along with free first refill and hotplate/stove.

## 2. Benefits & Financial Assistance
- **Free LPG Connection + First Refill & Stove:** Full security deposit waiver for cylinder and regulator + free first gas refill + free LPG stove.

## 3. Eligibility Criteria (Deterministic Rules)
- `gender eq female`
- `age gte 18`
- `annual_income lte 120000`

## 4. Required Documents Checklist
- [**Aadhaar Card**](../../../documents/identity/aadhaar-card.md) (Mandatory): Identity proof of woman applicant
- [**Ration Card**](../../../documents/income-wealth/ration-card.md) (Mandatory): Family composition proof
- [**Bank Passbook**](../../../documents/income-wealth/bank-passbook.md) (Mandatory): Aadhaar-linked bank account

## 5. Application Procedure
1. Verify that your citizen profile satisfies the eligibility rules listed above.
2. Ensure you have the required documents uploaded and verified in your Document Vault.
3. Access the official application portal: [https://pmuy.gov.in](https://pmuy.gov.in).
4. Submit the application and save your acknowledgement number.
