# Pradhan Mantri Awas Yojana - Gramin

## 1. Overview & Objective
Financial grant of up to ₹1,30,000 for houseless rural households and families living in kutcha/dilapidated homes to construct safe, pucca houses.

## 2. Benefits & Financial Assistance
- **₹1.2 Lakh to ₹1.3 Lakh Construction Grant:** Direct financial aid for building pucca house + 90 days MGNREGA unskilled labour wages + ₹12,000 toilet grant.

## 3. Eligibility Criteria (Deterministic Rules)
- `annual_income lte 180000`

## 4. Required Documents Checklist
- [**Aadhaar Card**](../../../documents/identity/aadhaar-card.md) (Mandatory): Identity proof
- [**Bank Passbook**](../../../documents/income-wealth/bank-passbook.md) (Mandatory): Bank account for DBT installments
- [**MGNREGA Job Card**](../../../documents/general-compliance/mgnrega-job-card.md) (Optional): Job card number for wage tracking

## 5. Application Procedure
1. Verify that your citizen profile satisfies the eligibility rules listed above.
2. Ensure you have the required documents uploaded and verified in your Document Vault.
3. Access the official application portal: [https://pmayg.nic.in](https://pmayg.nic.in).
4. Submit the application and save your acknowledgement number.
