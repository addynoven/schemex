# Gruha Lakshmi Scheme

## 1. Overview & Objective
Monthly financial assistance of ₹2,000 (₹24,000/year) transferred directly into bank accounts of female heads of household in Karnataka.

## 2. Benefits & Financial Assistance
- **₹2,000 Monthly Head of Household Grant:** ₹24,000 annual direct benefit transfer credited monthly into the woman head's bank account.

## 3. Eligibility Criteria (Deterministic Rules)
- `state eq Karnataka`
- `gender eq female`
- `age gte 18`

## 4. Required Documents Checklist
- [**Ration Card (Antyodaya / BPL / APL)**](../../../documents/income-wealth/ration-card-antyodaya-bpl-apl.md) (Mandatory): Ration card naming applicant as woman head of household
- [**Aadhaar Card of Woman Head**](../../../documents/identity/aadhaar-card-of-woman-head.md) (Mandatory): Identity proof
- [**Aadhaar-Linked Bank Passbook**](../../../documents/identity/aadhaar-linked-bank-passbook.md) (Mandatory): Bank account enabled with NPCI direct debit

## 5. Application Procedure
1. Verify that your citizen profile satisfies the eligibility rules listed above.
2. Ensure you have the required documents uploaded and verified in your Document Vault.
3. Access the official application portal: [https://sevasindhugs.karnataka.gov.in](https://sevasindhugs.karnataka.gov.in).
4. Submit the application and save your acknowledgement number.
