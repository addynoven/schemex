# Ayushman Bharat PM-JAY

## 1. Overview & Objective
World's largest health assurance scheme providing ₹5 Lakh cashless cover per family per year for secondary and tertiary care hospitalization.

## 2. Benefits & Financial Assistance
- **₹5 Lakh Cashless Health Cover:** Covers hospitalization, diagnostic tests, surgeries, medicines, and intensive care across 27,000+ empaneled hospitals.

## 3. Eligibility Criteria (Deterministic Rules)
- `annual_income lte 150000`

## 4. Required Documents Checklist
- [**Aadhaar Card**](../../../documents/identity/aadhaar-card.md) (Mandatory): Identity proof
- [**Ration Card**](../../../documents/income-wealth/ration-card.md) (Mandatory): Family membership / BPL card

## 5. Application Procedure
1. Verify that your citizen profile satisfies the eligibility rules listed above.
2. Ensure you have the required documents uploaded and verified in your Document Vault.
3. Access the official application portal: [https://beneficiary.nha.gov.in](https://beneficiary.nha.gov.in).
4. Submit the application and save your acknowledgement number.
