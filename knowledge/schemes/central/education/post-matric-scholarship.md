# Post-Matric Scholarship for Higher Education

## 1. Overview & Objective
100% compulsory tuition fee waiver and monthly maintenance allowance for students pursuing post-matriculation or post-secondary courses.

## 2. Benefits & Financial Assistance
- **Full Fee Reimbursement + Monthly Allowance:** Complete coverage of non-refundable college fees and monthly study allowance directly to student bank account.

## 3. Eligibility Criteria (Deterministic Rules)
- `age between 15-30`
- `annual_income lte 250000`

## 4. Required Documents Checklist
- [**Aadhaar Card**](../../../documents/identity/aadhaar-card.md) (Mandatory): Identity proof
- [**Income Certificate**](../../../documents/income-wealth/income-certificate.md) (Mandatory): Competent authority family income certificate
- [**Academic Marksheet**](../../../documents/education/academic-marksheet.md) (Mandatory): Previous qualifying exam marksheet
- [**College Admission Fee Receipt**](../../../documents/education/college-admission-fee-receipt.md) (Mandatory): Proof of active college enrollment

## 5. Application Procedure
1. Verify that your citizen profile satisfies the eligibility rules listed above.
2. Ensure you have the required documents uploaded and verified in your Document Vault.
3. Access the official application portal: [https://scholarships.gov.in](https://scholarships.gov.in).
4. Submit the application and save your acknowledgement number.
