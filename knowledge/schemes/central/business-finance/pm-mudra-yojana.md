# Pradhan Mantri Mudra Yojana

## 1. Overview & Objective
Collateral-free loans up to ₹10 Lakh for small manufacturing, processing, retail, and service enterprises under Shishu, Kishore, and Tarun categories.

## 2. Benefits & Financial Assistance
- **Collateral-Free Business Loans up to ₹10 Lakh:** Shishu (up to ₹50,000), Kishore (₹50,000 to ₹5 Lakh), Tarun (₹5 Lakh to ₹10 Lakh) with zero collateral requirements.

## 3. Eligibility Criteria (Deterministic Rules)
- `age gte 18`

## 4. Required Documents Checklist
- [**Aadhaar Card**](../../../documents/identity/aadhaar-card.md) (Mandatory): Identity proof
- [**PAN Card**](../../../documents/identity/pan-card.md) (Mandatory): Tax identity for business borrower
- [**Business Proof**](../../../documents/general-compliance/business-proof.md) (Mandatory): Udyam registration or shop establishment license
- [**Bank Statement**](../../../documents/income-wealth/bank-statement.md) (Mandatory): Last 6 months bank statement

## 5. Application Procedure
1. Verify that your citizen profile satisfies the eligibility rules listed above.
2. Ensure you have the required documents uploaded and verified in your Document Vault.
3. Access the official application portal: [https://www.udyamimitra.in](https://www.udyamimitra.in).
4. Submit the application and save your acknowledgement number.
