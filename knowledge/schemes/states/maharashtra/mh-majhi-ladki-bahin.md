# Mukhyamantri Majhi Ladki Bahin Yojana

## 1. Overview & Objective
Direct financial assistance of ₹1,500 per month (₹18,000/year) deposited directly into bank accounts of eligible resident women in Maharashtra aged 21-65.

## 2. Benefits & Financial Assistance
- **₹1,500 Monthly Financial Assistance:** ₹18,000 annual direct benefit transfer deposited into Aadhaar-linked bank accounts.

## 3. Eligibility Criteria (Deterministic Rules)
- `state eq Maharashtra`
- `gender eq female`
- `age between 21-65`
- `annual_income lte 250000`

## 4. Required Documents Checklist
- [**Aadhaar Card**](../../../documents/identity/aadhaar-card.md) (Mandatory): Mandatory identity proof
- [**Maharashtra Domicile Certificate**](../../../documents/social-category/maharashtra-domicile-certificate.md) (Mandatory): Domicile / Birth certificate proving 15+ years residency
- [**Ration Card (Yellow/Orange)**](../../../documents/income-wealth/ration-card-yelloworange.md) (Mandatory): Family income classification proof
- [**Bank Passbook**](../../../documents/income-wealth/bank-passbook.md) (Mandatory): Aadhaar-seeded bank account

## 5. Application Procedure
1. Verify that your citizen profile satisfies the eligibility rules listed above.
2. Ensure you have the required documents uploaded and verified in your Document Vault.
3. Access the official application portal: [https://ladakibahin.maharashtra.gov.in](https://ladakibahin.maharashtra.gov.in).
4. Submit the application and save your acknowledgement number.
