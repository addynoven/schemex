# Mukhyamantri Medhavi Vidyarthi Yojana (MMVY)

## 1. Overview & Objective
Complete tuition fee sponsorship for meritorious MP students pursuing engineering (IIT/NIT/JEE), medical (NEET), law (CLAT), or degree courses.

## 2. Benefits & Financial Assistance
- **100% Higher Education Tuition Fee Waiver:** Direct payment of entire college and course tuition fee directly to the educational institution by MP Government.

## 3. Eligibility Criteria (Deterministic Rules)
- `state eq Madhya Pradesh`
- `age between 16-25`
- `annual_income lte 600000`

## 4. Required Documents Checklist
- [**12th Class Marksheet**](../../../documents/education/12th-class-marksheet.md) (Mandatory): 70%+ in MP Board or 85%+ in CBSE/ICSE
- [**MP Domicile Certificate**](../../../documents/social-category/mp-domicile-certificate.md) (Mandatory): Mool Niwas Praman Patra
- [**Income Certificate**](../../../documents/income-wealth/income-certificate.md) (Mandatory): Family income certificate issued by Tehsildar
- [**College Admission Allotment Letter**](../../../documents/education/college-admission-allotment-letter.md) (Mandatory): Proof of active college enrollment

## 5. Application Procedure
1. Verify that your citizen profile satisfies the eligibility rules listed above.
2. Ensure you have the required documents uploaded and verified in your Document Vault.
3. Access the official application portal: [http://scholarshipportal.mp.nic.in](http://scholarshipportal.mp.nic.in).
4. Submit the application and save your acknowledgement number.
