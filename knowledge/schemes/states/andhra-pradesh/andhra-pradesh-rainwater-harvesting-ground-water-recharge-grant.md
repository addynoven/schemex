# Andhra Pradesh Rainwater Harvesting & Ground Water Recharge Grant

## 1. Overview & Objective
Subsidized rooftop rainwater harvesting system for individual residences. Implemented by Andhra Pradesh government for citizen welfare.

## 2. Benefits & Financial Assistance
- **Rainwater Harvesting & Ground Water Recharge Grant:** Subsidized rooftop rainwater harvesting system for individual residences. (₹15,000)

## 3. Eligibility Criteria (Deterministic Rules)
- `state eq Andhra Pradesh`
- `annual_income lte 500000`

## 4. Required Documents Checklist
- [**Aadhaar Card**](../../../documents/identity/aadhaar-card.md) (Mandatory): Official Aadhaar Card for verification
- [**Electricity / Property Bill**](../../../documents/property-land/electricity-property-bill.md) (Mandatory): Official Electricity / Property Bill for verification
- [**Bank Passbook**](../../../documents/income-wealth/bank-passbook.md) (Mandatory): Official Bank Passbook for verification

## 5. Application Procedure
1. Verify that your citizen profile satisfies the eligibility rules listed above.
2. Ensure you have the required documents uploaded and verified in your Document Vault.
3. Access the official application portal: [https://serviceonline.gov.in/andhra-pradesh](https://serviceonline.gov.in/andhra-pradesh).
4. Submit the application and save your acknowledgement number.
