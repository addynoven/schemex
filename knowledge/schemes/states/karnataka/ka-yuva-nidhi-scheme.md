# Yuva Nidhi Scheme

## 1. Overview & Objective
Monthly unemployment stipend of ₹3,000 for degree holders and ₹1,500 for diploma holders who completed education in the current academic year and remain unemployed.

## 2. Benefits & Financial Assistance
- **₹3,000 Monthly Unemployment Allowance:** Monthly financial allowance for up to 2 years while seeking employment or attending skill development training.

## 3. Eligibility Criteria (Deterministic Rules)
- `state eq Karnataka`
- `age between 18-35`
- `occupation eq unemployed`

## 4. Required Documents Checklist
- [**Degree / Diploma Certificate & Marksheet**](../../../documents/education/degree-diploma-certificate-marksheet.md) (Mandatory): Proof of passing degree or diploma
- [**Karnataka Domicile Certificate**](../../../documents/social-category/karnataka-domicile-certificate.md) (Mandatory): Residency proof in Karnataka for at least 6 years
- [**Aadhaar Card**](../../../documents/identity/aadhaar-card.md) (Mandatory): Identity proof
- [**Unemployment Self-Declaration**](../../../documents/income-wealth/unemployment-self-declaration.md) (Mandatory): Declaration of not being employed or self-employed

## 5. Application Procedure
1. Verify that your citizen profile satisfies the eligibility rules listed above.
2. Ensure you have the required documents uploaded and verified in your Document Vault.
3. Access the official application portal: [https://sevasindhugs.karnataka.gov.in](https://sevasindhugs.karnataka.gov.in).
4. Submit the application and save your acknowledgement number.
