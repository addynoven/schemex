# PM National Farm Mechanization & Rotavator Subsidy Phase 3

## 1. Overview & Objective
Central flagship program: Up to 50% subsidy on modern harvesters, rotavators, and custom hiring centers. Administered by Ministry of Agriculture & Farmers Welfare, Government of India.

## 2. Benefits & Financial Assistance
- **National Farm Mechanization & Rotavator Subsidy:** Up to 50% subsidy on modern harvesters, rotavators, and custom hiring centers. (₹60,000)

## 3. Eligibility Criteria (Deterministic Rules)
- `occupation eq Farmer`
- `annual_income lte 500000`

## 4. Required Documents Checklist
- [**Aadhaar Card**](../../../documents/identity/aadhaar-card.md) (Mandatory): National Aadhaar Card for verification
- [**Land Revenue Passbook (Khata/Khasra)**](../../../documents/income-wealth/land-revenue-passbook-khatakhasra.md) (Mandatory): National Land Revenue Passbook (Khata/Khasra) for verification
- [**Bank Passbook**](../../../documents/income-wealth/bank-passbook.md) (Mandatory): National Bank Passbook for verification

## 5. Application Procedure
1. Verify that your citizen profile satisfies the eligibility rules listed above.
2. Ensure you have the required documents uploaded and verified in your Document Vault.
3. Access the official application portal: [https://dbtbharat.gov.in/schemes/pm-national-farm-mechanization-rotavator-subsidy-phase-3](https://dbtbharat.gov.in/schemes/pm-national-farm-mechanization-rotavator-subsidy-phase-3).
4. Submit the application and save your acknowledgement number.
