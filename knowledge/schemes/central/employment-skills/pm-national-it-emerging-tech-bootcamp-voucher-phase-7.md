# PM National IT & Emerging Tech Bootcamp Voucher Phase 7

## 1. Overview & Objective
Central flagship program: Full tuition sponsorship for AI, Cloud, and Software certification bootcamps. Administered by Ministry of Agriculture & Farmers Welfare, Government of India.

## 2. Benefits & Financial Assistance
- **National IT & Emerging Tech Bootcamp Voucher:** Full tuition sponsorship for AI, Cloud, and Software certification bootcamps. (₹35,000)

## 3. Eligibility Criteria (Deterministic Rules)
- `occupation eq Student`
- `annual_income lte 500000`

## 4. Required Documents Checklist
- [**Aadhaar Card**](../../../documents/identity/aadhaar-card.md) (Mandatory): National Aadhaar Card for verification
- [**Educational Qualification Certificate**](../../../documents/education/educational-qualification-certificate.md) (Mandatory): National Educational Qualification Certificate for verification
- [**Bank Passbook**](../../../documents/income-wealth/bank-passbook.md) (Mandatory): National Bank Passbook for verification

## 5. Application Procedure
1. Verify that your citizen profile satisfies the eligibility rules listed above.
2. Ensure you have the required documents uploaded and verified in your Document Vault.
3. Access the official application portal: [https://dbtbharat.gov.in/schemes/pm-national-it-emerging-tech-bootcamp-voucher-phase-7](https://dbtbharat.gov.in/schemes/pm-national-it-emerging-tech-bootcamp-voucher-phase-7).
4. Submit the application and save your acknowledgement number.
