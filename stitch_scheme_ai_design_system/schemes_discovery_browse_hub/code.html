About the Scheme

Pradhan Mantri Kisan Samman Nidhi (PM-KISAN) is a flagship Central Sector scheme launched to augment the income of all landholding farmers' families in the country to support their financial needs for procuring various agricultural inputs and domestic necessities.

Under this initiative, the complete financial liability is borne by the Government of India. Funds are directly transferred into the bank accounts of beneficiaries through Aadhaar-enabled public financial management system (DBT) to prevent intermediaries.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is the PM Kisan mandatory eKYC deadline? expand_more

eKYC is mandatory for all PM-KISAN registered farmers. OTP based eKYC is available on PMKISAN Portal or through nearest CSC centers for biometric authentication.

Can both husband and wife receive PM-Kisan payments? expand_more

No. As per guidelines, only one beneficiary per 'farmer family' (comprising husband, wife, and minor children) is eligible to receive benefits.
