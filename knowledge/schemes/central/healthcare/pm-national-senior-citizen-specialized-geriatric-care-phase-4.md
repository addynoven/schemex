# PM National Senior Citizen Specialized Geriatric Care Phase 4

## 1. Overview & Objective
Central flagship program: Free cataract surgeries, hearing aids, and doorstep chronic care medicine. Administered by Ministry of Agriculture & Farmers Welfare, Government of India.

## 2. Benefits & Financial Assistance
- **National Senior Citizen Specialized Geriatric Care:** Free cataract surgeries, hearing aids, and doorstep chronic care medicine. (₹15,000)

## 3. Eligibility Criteria (Deterministic Rules)
- `age gte 60`

## 4. Required Documents Checklist
- [**Aadhaar Card**](../../../documents/identity/aadhaar-card.md) (Mandatory): National Aadhaar Card for verification
- [**Ration Card / Health Card**](../../../documents/income-wealth/ration-card-health-card.md) (Mandatory): National Ration Card / Health Card for verification
- [**Medical Prescription**](../../../documents/general-compliance/medical-prescription.md) (Mandatory): National Medical Prescription for verification

## 5. Application Procedure
1. Verify that your citizen profile satisfies the eligibility rules listed above.
2. Ensure you have the required documents uploaded and verified in your Document Vault.
3. Access the official application portal: [https://dbtbharat.gov.in/schemes/pm-national-senior-citizen-specialized-geriatric-care-phase-4](https://dbtbharat.gov.in/schemes/pm-national-senior-citizen-specialized-geriatric-care-phase-4).
4. Submit the application and save your acknowledgement number.
