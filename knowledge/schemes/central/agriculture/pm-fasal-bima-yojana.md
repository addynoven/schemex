# Pradhan Mantri Fasal Bima Yojana

## 1. Overview & Objective
Comprehensive insurance coverage against crop failure and yield loss due to non-preventable natural calamities, pests, and diseases at low premiums (1.5%-2%).

## 2. Benefits & Financial Assistance
- **Full Crop Damage Insurance Claim:** Direct settlement of claim amount into farmer bank accounts based on crop loss assessment.

## 3. Eligibility Criteria (Deterministic Rules)
- `occupation eq farmer`

## 4. Required Documents Checklist
- [**Aadhaar Card**](../../../documents/identity/aadhaar-card.md) (Mandatory): Identity proof
- [**Land Possession Certificate**](../../../documents/property-land/land-possession-certificate.md) (Mandatory): Khasra / Khatauni land record
- [**Sowing Certificate**](../../../documents/general-compliance/sowing-certificate.md) (Mandatory): Certificate from Patwari / Village Officer
- [**Bank Passbook**](../../../documents/income-wealth/bank-passbook.md) (Mandatory): Bank account for claim DBT

## 5. Application Procedure
1. Verify that your citizen profile satisfies the eligibility rules listed above.
2. Ensure you have the required documents uploaded and verified in your Document Vault.
3. Access the official application portal: [https://pmfby.gov.in](https://pmfby.gov.in).
4. Submit the application and save your acknowledgement number.
