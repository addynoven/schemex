# West Bengal Phase-3 IT & Emerging Tech Bootcamp Voucher

## 1. Overview & Objective
Full tuition sponsorship for AI, Cloud, and Software certification bootcamps. Implemented by West Bengal government for citizen welfare.

## 2. Benefits & Financial Assistance
- **IT & Emerging Tech Bootcamp Voucher:** Full tuition sponsorship for AI, Cloud, and Software certification bootcamps. (₹35,000)

## 3. Eligibility Criteria (Deterministic Rules)
- `state eq West Bengal`
- `occupation eq Student`
- `annual_income lte 500000`

## 4. Required Documents Checklist
- [**Aadhaar Card**](../../../documents/identity/aadhaar-card.md) (Mandatory): Official Aadhaar Card for verification
- [**Educational Qualification Certificate**](../../../documents/education/educational-qualification-certificate.md) (Mandatory): Official Educational Qualification Certificate for verification
- [**Bank Passbook**](../../../documents/income-wealth/bank-passbook.md) (Mandatory): Official Bank Passbook for verification

## 5. Application Procedure
1. Verify that your citizen profile satisfies the eligibility rules listed above.
2. Ensure you have the required documents uploaded and verified in your Document Vault.
3. Access the official application portal: [https://serviceonline.gov.in/west-bengal](https://serviceonline.gov.in/west-bengal).
4. Submit the application and save your acknowledgement number.
