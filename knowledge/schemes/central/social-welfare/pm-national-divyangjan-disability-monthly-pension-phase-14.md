# PM National Divyangjan Disability Monthly Pension Phase 14

## 1. Overview & Objective
Central flagship program: Monthly financial support and motorized tricycle allowance for disabled persons. Administered by Ministry of Agriculture & Farmers Welfare, Government of India.

## 2. Benefits & Financial Assistance
- **National Divyangjan Disability Monthly Pension:** Monthly financial support and motorized tricycle allowance for disabled persons. (₹18,000)

## 3. Eligibility Criteria (Deterministic Rules)
- `annual_income lte 250000`

## 4. Required Documents Checklist
- [**Aadhaar Card**](../../../documents/identity/aadhaar-card.md) (Mandatory): National Aadhaar Card for verification
- [**Income Certificate**](../../../documents/income-wealth/income-certificate.md) (Mandatory): National Income Certificate for verification
- [**Bank Passbook**](../../../documents/income-wealth/bank-passbook.md) (Mandatory): National Bank Passbook for verification
- [**Disability/Caste Certificate**](../../../documents/social-category/disabilitycaste-certificate.md) (Optional): National Disability/Caste Certificate for verification

## 5. Application Procedure
1. Verify that your citizen profile satisfies the eligibility rules listed above.
2. Ensure you have the required documents uploaded and verified in your Document Vault.
3. Access the official application portal: [https://dbtbharat.gov.in/schemes/pm-national-divyangjan-disability-monthly-pension-phase-14](https://dbtbharat.gov.in/schemes/pm-national-divyangjan-disability-monthly-pension-phase-14).
4. Submit the application and save your acknowledgement number.
