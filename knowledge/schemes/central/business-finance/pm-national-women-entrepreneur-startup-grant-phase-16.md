# PM National Women Entrepreneur Startup Grant Phase 16

## 1. Overview & Objective
Central flagship program: Seed funding capital grant for innovative women-founded MSME ventures. Administered by Ministry of Agriculture & Farmers Welfare, Government of India.

## 2. Benefits & Financial Assistance
- **National Women Entrepreneur Startup Grant:** Seed funding capital grant for innovative women-founded MSME ventures. (₹100,000)

## 3. Eligibility Criteria (Deterministic Rules)
- `occupation eq Business`
- `gender eq Female`

## 4. Required Documents Checklist
- [**Udyam Registration Certificate**](../../../documents/income-wealth/udyam-registration-certificate.md) (Mandatory): National Udyam Registration Certificate for verification
- [**PAN Card**](../../../documents/identity/pan-card.md) (Mandatory): National PAN Card for verification
- [**Aadhaar Card**](../../../documents/identity/aadhaar-card.md) (Mandatory): National Aadhaar Card for verification
- [**Bank Account Statement**](../../../documents/income-wealth/bank-account-statement.md) (Mandatory): National Bank Account Statement for verification

## 5. Application Procedure
1. Verify that your citizen profile satisfies the eligibility rules listed above.
2. Ensure you have the required documents uploaded and verified in your Document Vault.
3. Access the official application portal: [https://dbtbharat.gov.in/schemes/pm-national-women-entrepreneur-startup-grant-phase-16](https://dbtbharat.gov.in/schemes/pm-national-women-entrepreneur-startup-grant-phase-16).
4. Submit the application and save your acknowledgement number.
