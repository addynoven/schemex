# PM National Technical & Polytechnic Skill Stipend Phase 6

## 1. Overview & Objective
Central flagship program: Monthly allowance for students pursuing diploma and industrial training. Administered by Ministry of Agriculture & Farmers Welfare, Government of India.

## 2. Benefits & Financial Assistance
- **National Technical & Polytechnic Skill Stipend:** Monthly allowance for students pursuing diploma and industrial training. (₹12,000)

## 3. Eligibility Criteria (Deterministic Rules)
- `occupation eq Student`
- `annual_income lte 300000`

## 4. Required Documents Checklist
- [**Aadhaar Card**](../../../documents/identity/aadhaar-card.md) (Mandatory): National Aadhaar Card for verification
- [**Marksheet / Grade Certificate**](../../../documents/education/marksheet-grade-certificate.md) (Mandatory): National Marksheet / Grade Certificate for verification
- [**Income Certificate**](../../../documents/income-wealth/income-certificate.md) (Mandatory): National Income Certificate for verification
- [**Bank Passbook**](../../../documents/income-wealth/bank-passbook.md) (Mandatory): National Bank Passbook for verification

## 5. Application Procedure
1. Verify that your citizen profile satisfies the eligibility rules listed above.
2. Ensure you have the required documents uploaded and verified in your Document Vault.
3. Access the official application portal: [https://dbtbharat.gov.in/schemes/pm-national-technical-polytechnic-skill-stipend-phase-6](https://dbtbharat.gov.in/schemes/pm-national-technical-polytechnic-skill-stipend-phase-6).
4. Submit the application and save your acknowledgement number.
