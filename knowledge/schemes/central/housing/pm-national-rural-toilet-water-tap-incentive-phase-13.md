# PM National Rural Toilet & Water Tap Incentive Phase 13

## 1. Overview & Objective
Central flagship program: Financial assistance for individual household latrines and clean piped tap. Administered by Ministry of Agriculture & Farmers Welfare, Government of India.

## 2. Benefits & Financial Assistance
- **National Rural Toilet & Water Tap Incentive:** Financial assistance for individual household latrines and clean piped tap. (₹12,000)

## 3. Eligibility Criteria (Deterministic Rules)
- `annual_income lte 200000`

## 4. Required Documents Checklist
- [**Aadhaar Card**](../../../documents/identity/aadhaar-card.md) (Mandatory): National Aadhaar Card for verification
- [**Land Possession Certificate**](../../../documents/property-land/land-possession-certificate.md) (Mandatory): National Land Possession Certificate for verification
- [**Income Certificate**](../../../documents/income-wealth/income-certificate.md) (Mandatory): National Income Certificate for verification
- [**Bank Passbook**](../../../documents/income-wealth/bank-passbook.md) (Mandatory): National Bank Passbook for verification

## 5. Application Procedure
1. Verify that your citizen profile satisfies the eligibility rules listed above.
2. Ensure you have the required documents uploaded and verified in your Document Vault.
3. Access the official application portal: [https://dbtbharat.gov.in/schemes/pm-national-rural-toilet-water-tap-incentive-phase-13](https://dbtbharat.gov.in/schemes/pm-national-rural-toilet-water-tap-incentive-phase-13).
4. Submit the application and save your acknowledgement number.
