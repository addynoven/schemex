# Indira Gandhi National Old Age Pension Scheme

## 1. Overview & Objective
Monthly financial assistance for senior citizens living below poverty line to ensure dignified social security.

## 2. Benefits & Financial Assistance
- **Monthly Cash Pension:** ₹1,000 to ₹2,000 per month credited directly into beneficiary bank accounts.

## 3. Eligibility Criteria (Deterministic Rules)
- `age gte 60`
- `annual_income lte 100000`

## 4. Required Documents Checklist
- [**Aadhaar Card**](../../../documents/identity/aadhaar-card.md) (Mandatory): Identity proof
- [**Age Proof Certificate**](../../../documents/general-compliance/age-proof-certificate.md) (Mandatory): Birth certificate or voter card showing age 60+
- [**Income Certificate**](../../../documents/income-wealth/income-certificate.md) (Mandatory): BPL card or Tehsildar income certificate
- [**Bank Passbook**](../../../documents/income-wealth/bank-passbook.md) (Mandatory): Bank account for pension DBT

## 5. Application Procedure
1. Verify that your citizen profile satisfies the eligibility rules listed above.
2. Ensure you have the required documents uploaded and verified in your Document Vault.
3. Access the official application portal: [https://nsap.nic.in](https://nsap.nic.in).
4. Submit the application and save your acknowledgement number.
