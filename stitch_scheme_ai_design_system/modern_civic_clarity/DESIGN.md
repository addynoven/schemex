## Brand & Style

This design system establishes an unencumbered, high-trust digital interface dedicated to citizen welfare, institutional transparency, and administrative clarity. Moving strictly away from dense bureaucratic layouts and cosmetic visual clutter, the interface adheres to a refined **Minimalist Civic Utility** philosophy. 

### Core Ethos
- **Essentialist & Uncluttered:** Prioritize primary tasks, eligibility criteria, and disbursement realities. Strictly eliminate superficial ornamentation, decorative tags, micro-labels, and redundant trust badges.
- **Dignified & Authoritative:** Built on structural precision, purposeful negative space, and deep forest emerald accents that command confidence without shouting.
- **Calm, High-Efficiency Scanning:** The interface respects the user's attention. Complex statutory policies are distilled into clean typographical hierarchies rather than fragmented boxes and decorative pills.

### Anti-Patterns & Prohibitions
To preserve absolute clarity and prevent visual exhaustion, the following are strictly prohibited:
- **No Decorative Pills or Generic Chips:** Do not place non-interactive status pills, aesthetic category badges, or minor metadata inside rounded tags. Category information belongs in clean semantic text.
- **No Redundant Compliance Badges:** Never display vanity or redundant trust badges (such as "DPDPA Compliant", "SSL Secured", "100% Verified", or generic official shield icons) across headers, hero sections, or cards. Trust is conveyed through layout rigor and institutional domain authenticity, not self-congratulatory stamps.
- **No Header & Card Micro-Labels:** Prohibit tiny, scattered uppercase tags (e.g., "SCHEME TYPE", "STATUS: ACTIVE", "NOTICE", "OFFICIAL PORTAL") preceding headlines. Headings and content must stand on their own semantic weight.

## Colors

The color palette centers on a disciplined forest emerald primary supported by architectural neutrals and generous white space. Chromatic saturation is deployed with restraint—reserved solely for primary actions, critical state changes, and key monetary metrics.

### Key Palettes
- **Primary (`#0E6245`):** The institutional anchor. Used exclusively for primary interactive controls, focal monetary summaries, and active navigation indicators.
- **Secondary (`#1F6C3A`):** Used sparingly for secondary positive confirmations and completed pathway indicators.
- **Neutral (`#131B2E`):** The foundational ink color ensuring optimal contrast and readability across long policy copy.

### Functional Neutrals & Surfaces
- **Canvas (`#FAF8FF`):** A soft, low-glare background providing subtle contrast against foreground cards.
- **Card Surface (`#FFFFFF`):** Pure white field reserved for actionable content, tables, and form groups.
- **Borders (`#DAE2FD` / `#BFC9C1`):** Hairline structural demarcations used only where whitespace alone cannot separate complex tables or dense form groups.
- **Muted Text (`#3F4943`):** Subdued text for supportive descriptions and tabular meta-labels.

### Semantic Alerts
Color is never used decoratively. Critical alerts (disqualification, overdue deadlines) use direct typographic contrast or minimalist inline text indicators rather than vibrant, distracting pills.

## Typography

Typography delivers hierarchy and clarity through deliberate sizing, strict scale pruning, and calibrated vertical rhythm. By streamlining type weights and avoiding hyper-bold decorative treatments, the page achieves an effortless editorial cadence.

### Rules of Engagement
- **No Scattered Micro-Labels:** Do not use `label-sm` to create auxiliary category flags or uppercase eyebrow headers above page titles. Instead, incorporate structural context naturally into the primary header or lead paragraph.
- **Numerical Focus:** Direct Benefit Transfer (DBT) amounts, application IDs, and subsidy values use `code-mono` or standard tabular figures without artificial containment pills or colored halos.
- **Hierarchy via Scale and Space:** Distinguish card levels and sections using margins and font scale (`headline-md` vs. `body-md`), never via background tag containers.

## Layout & Spacing

Whitespace is an active structural element, not mere background. By giving content ample breathing room, visual noise is naturally dissipated without relying on box-in-box nesting or artificial dividers.

### Grid & Canvas Structure
- **Desktop (1024px+):** 12-column layout with an uncluttered `max-width` of 1200px. Exterior canvas margin is generous at `3rem` (`margin`), with `2rem` (`gutter`) column separation.
- **Tablet (768px - 1023px):** 8-column layout with `2rem` canvas margins and `1.5rem` gutters.
- **Mobile (320px - 767px):** 4-column layout with `1.25rem` (`margin-mobile`) canvas margins and `1rem` (`gutter-mobile`) column gaps. Content reflows into clear, unfragmented single-column flows.

### Spatial Discipline
- **Section Isolation:** Distinct sections and stages within an application process are separated by `space-xl` (40px) rather than bordered partitions.
- **Inner Padding:** Cards utilize generous internal padding (`space-lg` to `space-xl`), allowing primary typography to breathe without cramped margins.
- **Proximity as Meaning:** Related elements (e.g., input label and its field) sit closely (`space-xs` to `space-sm`), while unrelated blocks sit at least `space-lg` apart.

## Elevation & Depth

To maintain a crisp, modern aesthetic, the design system avoids heavy drop shadows, fuzzy elevation tiers, and frosted glass treatments. Depth is achieved purely through flat tonal planes and subtle hairline borders.

### Depth Hierarchy
- **Canvas Base:** `#FAF8FF` provides a soft, unified plane.
- **Structural Content Panels:** Pure white (`#FFFFFF`) surfaces resting flat against the canvas. Surfaces rely on hairline outline boundaries (`#DAE2FD`) rather than drop shadows.
- **Overlays & Dialogs:** When modal interruption is required, cards elevate using a disciplined, low-opacity ambient blur (`0 12px 32px -4px rgba(19, 27, 46, 0.06)`), framed by a crisp 1px `#BFC9C1` edge.
- **Interactive State Depth:** Focus and selection are indicated strictly through a direct 2px border accent in `#0E6245`. Shadows are not added on hover or focus.

## Shapes

The design system adopts a calibrated **Soft** geometric shape language (`roundedness: 1`), prioritizing architectural permanence over toy-like curves.

- **Base Corner Radius (`0.25rem` / 4px):** Applied to form inputs, selection controls, and inline buttons.
- **Medium Panels (`0.5rem` / 8px):** Applied to content cards, data tables, and structural containers.
- **Strict Prohibition of Rounded-Full (Pills):** Pills (`rounded-full` or 9999px radius) are expressly prohibited for badges, tags, buttons, and status indicators. All interactive and structural components maintain a restrained, rectangular geometry with minimal corner softening.

## Components

All components are stripped of ornamental dressing to focus directly on legibility, accessibility, and purposeful interaction.

### Buttons
- **Primary Action:** Solid forest emerald (`#0E6245`), crisp white typography (`#FFFFFF`), `font-weight: 600`, corner radius `0.25rem`. Padding: `12px 24px`. Flat finish, zero drop shadow. Hover state: `#004831`.
- **Secondary / Outline Action:** Transparent background with a 1px border in `#0E6245`, text in `#0E6245`. Hover shifts background to `#F2F3FF`.
- **Tertiary / Link Action:** No border, no background. Text in `#0E6245` with subtle underline on hover.

### Chips & Badges Policy
- **Elimination of Decorative Pills:** Decorative chips, status badges, and pill tags are banned.
- **Status Communication:** Convey state through plain, inline typographic markers (e.g., standard `body-bold` or `label-md` accompanied by an inline 6px square status indicator: `#1F6C3A` for active/approved, `#BA1A1A` for action required). Never encase status text inside colored lozenges or pills.
- **Filter Controls:** When user-selectable filters are required, use clean segmented tabs or subtle rectangular toggles with standard `0.25rem` corners—never rounded pill capsules.

### Form Inputs & Checkboxes
- **Input Fields:** Pure white background, 1px border `#BFC9C1`, radius `0.25rem`, height 44px. Text in `#131B2E`. Focus state transitions crisply to a 2px `#0E6245` border with zero glow or blur.
- **Labels:** Set directly in `label-md` (`#131B2E`) without extraneous asterisks or decorative tags. Helper text is placed below in `label-sm` (`#3F4943`).
- **Checkboxes & Radios:** 18px square (or circle for radios) with a 1.5px border `#BFC9C1`. Active state fills solid `#0E6245` with an unembellished white glyph.

### Cards & Data Lists
- **Content Cards:** Clean white surfaces framed by a hairline `#DAE2FD` border with generous internal padding (`space-lg` to `space-xl`).
- **Header Discipline:** Card headers contain only the title (`headline-md` or `headline-sm`) and relevant contextual body text. Never place badge clusters, category chips, or compliance marks along the card perimeter.
- **Data Rows & Lists:** Separate multi-attribute data using simple horizontal hairline rules and generous vertical spacing rather than nested sub-cards or boxed capsules.
