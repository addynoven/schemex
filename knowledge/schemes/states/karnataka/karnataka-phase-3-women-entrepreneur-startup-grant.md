# Karnataka Phase-3 Women Entrepreneur Startup Grant

## 1. Overview & Objective
Seed funding capital grant for innovative women-founded MSME ventures. Implemented by Karnataka government for citizen welfare.

## 2. Benefits & Financial Assistance
- **Women Entrepreneur Startup Grant:** Seed funding capital grant for innovative women-founded MSME ventures. (₹100,000)

## 3. Eligibility Criteria (Deterministic Rules)
- `state eq Karnataka`
- `occupation eq Business`
- `gender eq Female`

## 4. Required Documents Checklist
- [**Udyam Registration Certificate**](../../../documents/income-wealth/udyam-registration-certificate.md) (Mandatory): Official Udyam Registration Certificate for verification
- [**PAN Card**](../../../documents/identity/pan-card.md) (Mandatory): Official PAN Card for verification
- [**Aadhaar Card**](../../../documents/identity/aadhaar-card.md) (Mandatory): Official Aadhaar Card for verification
- [**Bank Account Statement**](../../../documents/income-wealth/bank-account-statement.md) (Mandatory): Official Bank Account Statement for verification

## 5. Application Procedure
1. Verify that your citizen profile satisfies the eligibility rules listed above.
2. Ensure you have the required documents uploaded and verified in your Document Vault.
3. Access the official application portal: [https://serviceonline.gov.in/karnataka](https://serviceonline.gov.in/karnataka).
4. Submit the application and save your acknowledgement number.
